COMMERCIAL WATER RECAPTURE

A deep clean.
Carefully collected.

Our high-quality commercial water recapture system uses suction to collect the wastewater generated during pressure washing, helping keep wash water and the dirt it carries out of storm drains.

For commercial properties, sidewalks and parking areas, ask Steve and Aidan about water recapture when planning your cleaning.

Discuss your commercial property
HOW IT WORKS

Wash. Capture. Contain.

As surfaces are cleaned, suction equipment recaptures the wash water for collection instead of letting it flow toward storm drains.
